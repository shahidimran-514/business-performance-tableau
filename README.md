## 🔍 Key Insights

**Top Customers**
- Mariam Shah leads at $63,242 in purchases
- Top 10 customers are highly concentrated
  in similar spend ranges $52K — $63K
- Relatively balanced top customer distribution

**Category Performance**
- Electronics dominates profit at $1,146,625
- Furniture second at $310,077
- Clothing lowest at $95,204
- Electronics is 73% of total profit

**Regional Performance**
- East region leads at $2,978,933
- Relatively balanced across all 4 regions
- North is weakest at $1,971,085
- East vs North gap is $1M opportunity

**Sales Trend**
- Peak performance in April at $1,111,341
- Sharp summer decline from May onward
- August lowest at $619,775
- Recovery beginning in October through December

**Discount vs Profit Analysis**
- Electronics maintains high profit
  despite high discount levels
- Clothing shows low profit at all discount levels
- Furniture performs moderately
